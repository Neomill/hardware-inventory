# Current Business Workflow

## Business Hours

### Opening the Store

**Time:** Approximately 7:00 AM

### Steps

1. The cashier arrives first and opens the hardware store.
2. The cashier prepares the cashier area.
3. Cash drawer is prepared with starting cash.
4. Inventory is NOT counted every morning.

---

# Customer Purchase Workflow

## Step 1 - Customer Arrives

A customer enters the hardware store looking for construction materials or hardware items.

↓

## Step 2 - Helper Assists Customer

A helper approaches the customer and asks what products they need.

The helper is responsible for:

- Finding the requested products.
- Checking product availability.
- Looking up the selling price.
- Writing every requested item on a paper list.

The paper contains:

- Product name
- Quantity
- Unit price
- Total price (optional)

This paper acts as a temporary sales slip.

↓

## Step 3 - Item Preparation

The helper gathers all requested items from the shelves.

If an item is unavailable, the helper informs the customer.

↓

## Step 4 - Cashier Processing

The helper brings the following to the cashier:

- Prepared products
- Paper sales slip
- Customer payment

The cashier:

1. Reviews the paper.
2. Computes the total amount.
3. Receives payment.
4. Computes the customer's change.
5. Returns the change.
6. Gives the products to the customer.

Official Receipts (OR) are issued only when requested by the customer.

Otherwise, the handwritten paper is used as the transaction record.

↓

## Step 5 - End of Transaction

Customer leaves the store.

No inventory is updated immediately.

The handwritten paper is kept for later recording.

---

# Inventory Update Workflow

Inventory is NOT updated in real time.

Instead:

1. The store closes.
2. The cashier reviews all paper sales.
3. Inventory notebook is updated manually.
4. Spreadsheet (if applicable) is updated.

Current stock is therefore delayed until the end of the day.

---

# Stock Receiving Workflow

Stock deliveries normally happen after business hours.

## Steps

1. Delivery truck arrives.
2. Staff unload products.
3. Products are placed on shelves.
4. New stock is recorded manually.
5. Selling prices may be adjusted depending on the new purchase cost.

Inventory is updated after receiving is completed.

---

# Customer Ledger Workflow (Version 1)

Version 1 supports a basic customer ledger.

## Credit Sale

1. Customer purchases items.
2. Customer cannot fully pay.
3. Remaining balance is recorded.
4. Customer account shows outstanding balance.

## Payment

1. Customer returns.
2. Customer makes a partial or full payment.
3. Remaining balance is updated.

A customer may have multiple unpaid balances simultaneously.

Detailed ledger features will be expanded in Version 2.

---

# Store Closing

At the end of the day:

- Inventory notebook is updated.
- Sales papers are organized.
- Ledger is updated.
- Newly received stock is recorded (if any).
- Store is closed.
