# Business Observations

## Products

- Products do not use barcodes.
- Many prices are remembered by experienced staff.
- Some products are sold by piece.
- Some products are sold by box.
- Some products are sold by meter.
- Some products are purchased in sacks but sold by kilogram.
- Product availability is checked manually.

---

## Inventory

Inventory is updated only after store closing.

Current stock shown during the day may not reflect actual remaining stock.

---

## Sales

Sales are initially recorded on handwritten paper.

Official Receipts are issued only when requested.

---

## Store Operations

Owner does not open the store.

Cashier operates independently during the morning.

Helpers assist customers before the cashier handles payment.

---

## Stock Receiving

Stock deliveries usually happen after business hours.

Products are unloaded from delivery trucks and placed directly on shelves before inventory is updated.
