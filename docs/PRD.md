# Hardware Store Management System

## Purpose

The Hardware Store Management System is a digital platform designed to replace the store's paper-based inventory, sales, and ledger processes.

The system aims to simplify daily operations, reduce manual work, improve inventory accuracy, and provide real-time visibility into sales and stock levels while remaining simple enough for employees with minimal computer experience.

Version 1 focuses on supporting a single hardware store with one cashier and offline-first operation.


## Current Problems

The store currently relies on notebooks, handwritten sales slips, and manual spreadsheets.

This creates several operational issues:

- Inventory is updated only after closing.
- Stock levels are often inaccurate during the day.
- Sales are manually calculated.
- Customer ledger is handwritten.
- Daily reports require manual computation.
- Paper records can be misplaced.
- Product prices require manual lookup.

## Business Goals

- Digitize daily operations.
- Reduce inventory mistakes.
- Reduce cashier workload.
- Provide real-time inventory.
- Automatically generate sales reports.
- Improve customer ledger management.
- Keep the application usable even without internet access.

## Success Metrics

- Complete a sale in under two minutes.
- Search products in less than five seconds.
- Eliminate nightly inventory encoding.
- Automatically generate daily sales reports.
- Reduce manual calculations.
